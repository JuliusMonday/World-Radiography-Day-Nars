# Question bank: sources and review notes

The quiz has 830 questions: 221 hand-written fact questions and 609 calculation questions whose answers are computed from standard formulas (mAs, inverse square law, density maintenance, 15% rule, magnification, grid ratio, half-value layers, unit conversions, radioactive decay, pixel size, spatial resolution, heat units, CT windows).

Questions that depend on regulations, guidelines or historical dates were checked against the sources below (November 2026). Standard textbook facts (anatomy counts, basic physics, common projections) follow the usual radiography texts.

## Nigeria

- **Radiographers Registration Board of Nigeria:** Radiographers (Registration, etc.) Act 1987, Cap R1 LFN 2004. The Board was established to control the practice of radiography, keep the register, set standards, conduct examinations and (Section 18) set up the Nigerian Institute of Radiographers. [Laws of Nigeria (PLAC)](https://placng.org/lawsofnigeria/print.php?sn=473). The RRBN portal (portal.rrbn.gov.ng) was unreachable when checked.
- **Dose limits in Nigeria:** NNRA applies 20 mSv a year for occupational exposure under the Nigeria Basic Ionizing Radiation Regulations 2003, in line with ICRP and the IAEA Basic Safety Standards. [IAEA conference paper on Nigerian occupational monitoring](https://conferences.iaea.org/event/276/contributions/24455), [NNRA ORPAS report](https://nnra.gov.ng/nnra/controller/includes/download.php?f=controller%2Fpages%2FgeneralItem%2FResourcesFiles%2F102.pdf&fc=Nigeria++ORPAS+Report.pdf)

## Radiation protection

- **ICRP Publication 103 limits** (occupational 20 mSv/yr averaged over 5 years, max 50 mSv in a year; public 1 mSv; skin/hands/feet 500 mSv; public skin 50 mSv; about 1 mSv to the embryo/fetus after a declared pregnancy). [ICRPaedia: Dose limits](https://icrpaedia.org/Dose_limits)
- **Lens of the eye: 20 mSv/yr** (ICRP 2011 Seoul Statement, adopted in IAEA GSR Part 3). [US NRC issue paper](https://www.nrc.gov/docs/ml1408/ml14084a341.pdf), [IAEA lens of the eye](https://nucleus.iaea.org/sites/orpnet/resources/outreachmaterial/SitePages/Lens-of-the-eye.pdf)
- **Mobile radiography, about 2 m from tube and patient** (wording says "common guidance"; local rules apply). [SESLHD ward radiation safety](https://www.seslhd.health.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/documents/SESLHDPR%20539%20-%20Radiation%20Safety%20in%20Ward%20Areas.pdf), [Health Physics Society](https://hps.org/publicinformation/ate/q14699/)
